# Jaipur Gems Digital

You are an award-winning luxury web designer and front-end developer.

Design an ultra-premium, cinematic website for a heritage jewellery house based in Jaipur, India.

The objective is NOT to sell jewellery online.

The objective is to create a breathtaking digital experience that builds trust, showcases craftsmanship, and converts visitors into WhatsApp enquiries and showroom appointments.

The website should feel comparable to Cartier, Van Cleef & Arpels, Tiffany & Co., Bulgari and Sabyasachi in terms of elegance and storytelling, while celebrating the royal heritage of Jaipur.
